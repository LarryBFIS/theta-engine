STEP 1 · IDENTIFY — the opportunity radar

Every hour, the engine scans 40 of the most liquid stocks & ETFs and asks one question: where is the market overpaying for insurance? When option premium is statistically rich, selling that insurance is a positive-expectancy business.
Tech: real-time implied-volatility rank screening, lognormal probability-of-profit modeling, executable-price expectancy (bid/ask-crossed), liquidity microstructure gates, macro-event calendar awareness (FOMC/earnings), and a VIX regime classifier with automatic risk stand-down.

STEP 2 · STRUCTURE & SIZE — engineering the trade

It then designs a defined-risk trade — put spread (VP), call spread (VC) or iron condor (IC) — where the maximum possible loss is known and capped before entry, and sizes it so no single trade can meaningfully damage the account.
Tech: trend-filtered structure selection (20/50-day MA), portfolio delta-balancing, capital-at-risk caps (≤10% max loss, ≤50% buying-power utilization), volatility-skewed condor wings, contract sizing against a $20k institutional-style paper sandbox.

STEP 3 · EXECUTE & MONITOR — the 10-minute heartbeat

Trades are opened in a real-price paper book (and flagged for live replication). Every 10 minutes the engine re-prices every position at live market quotes and decides: hold, take profit, or cut.
Tech: executable-fill simulation (entries at the crossed spread, exits at ask/bid, exchange fees deducted), automated management — 50% profit harvest, 1.5× credit stop-loss, 21-DTE time exit, same-day close for 0DTE intraday structures, event-driven exits for IV-crush trades.

STEP 4 · P&L TRUTH — radical accounting honesty

Every dollar is reconciled: opening capital → current value → profit, split into realized, unrealized and fees. The paper book is engineered to never flatter — real money would do as well or better.
Tech: broker-ledger reconciliation from raw transaction history, conservative mark-to-market, expectancy & win-rate telemetry feeding a probability-calibration loop.

STEP 5 · LEARN — the investor model

The system maintains a living profile of its operator — risk tolerance, decisions, preferences — so every recommendation fits Jay Vekaria's strategy, and the edge is proven on paper before capital scales into it.
Tech: human-in-the-loop LIVE approval workflow, push-notification trade tickets, staged autonomy roadmap → fully automated execution once the measured edge clears statistical significance.
